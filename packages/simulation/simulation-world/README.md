## Finite Persona review and hiring market

Opt-in scenes install `organization.hiring-market/v1` together with their hiring operation. `hiring-market` commands retain finite Persona drafts, vacancies, independent candidate applications and immutable offer revisions in Native results. Draft edits consume a maximum of sixteen revisions, retain the original author and previous hash, record the current human or actor editor, and invalidate review; acceptance freezes the original template selection. Generated hiring in these scenes requires the same accepted draft body and exact `personaDraft` ID/revision/hash. The original plan and birth profile retain that reference to its immutable source. Inspection and human review perform no inference and expose no private Session material.

A candidate needs an explicit current `eligible-for-hire` relation and a scoped market operation grant. This allows market decisions without an assignment; it does not grant organizational knowledge access or other operations. Offers retain the original existing-person or reviewed generated-candidate hiring plan, candidate, vacancy, fee, approvals, deadline and terms hash. Delivery is not acceptance. Only the original candidate's own decision can accept or decline. Direct human `hire-existing` management is refused in market scenes; the employer must retain its own actor decision. Employer counteroffers create a new revision; stale, impersonated, late and incompatible acceptances reject at Native commit.

Vacancies freeze `on-proposal` or `on-acceptance` reservation policy. The former atomically reserves the original fee and position when the offer is issued, with validity bounded by the original hold; the latter reserves them when the candidate accepts. Neither policy substitutes reservation for consent. Generated candidates already retain their independent preparation identity and fee obligation before an offer. Later expiry or case cancellation cannot release an accepted obligation. Hiring reuses the same Person and Session, commits the original charge once and retains retry identity. These contracts do not implement recurring payroll: `compensation: one-time-only` cannot imply future salary. Review and offer history is available through the bounded Product `hiring-market` read family; case and Persona content permissions remain independent. Authenticated human `hiring-market` management accepts only finite draft/review commands and records a user source, never a candidate decision.

Reviewed generation first confirms an independent ready Person, original Participant/Session, and candidate market permissions under its preparation maintenance. Its own Session applies and accepts the exact delivered offer before a separate commit maintenance publishes assignment and payment. Refusal, withdrawal, or explicit expiry cancels the original job and releases its unaccepted fee while retaining the independent person and historical birth charges. Unknown preparation IO retains the original identity and funds until canonical reconciliation; process recovery resumes that owner without regenerating the Persona or inferring consent.

Bounded World message contexts record a `world-message-route` with the delivered operation, sender Entity ID, Participant ID and original channel. Reply through `replyTarget.entityId`; the envelope Participant ID is not the simulation address. A fresh retry includes the last rejected submission code/hash/attempt and explicitly states that no message effect occurred. Retained submitted, accepted or cancelled windows are committed or closed without redispatching their original model input.

## Model Experience

### Actor context and decision

#### What the model sees

Every selected view is recorded through `simulation/context-view`. Identity contains only that actor's profile and public organization; authority contains its assignments and relevant capacity usage. Native appends exact request/planning or result/first-action facts. Ordinary received messages follow canonical Team delivery. The driver-owned instructions are:

##### Verbatim decision instructions

```markdown
Use simulation_observe, simulation_stage and simulation_submit. Submit only actions allowed by the recorded context. An ordinary message may conclude with no further effects.
```

##### Verbatim commitment instructions

```markdown
Accepted effects remain pending until Native execution. A submitted plan is not employment; notify only recorded results.
```

#### Token effect

Only selected real Agent decisions infer. All director, requester and newcomer calls consume the same Native token/decision allowances and Team model pool. Identity provisioning and replay do not infer; the context byte budget can refuse required content. The pure `./required-context` entry renders the same fixed identity, authority and commitment blocks for runtime and preflight. Allocation-free initial sizing excludes later input/goals, system/tools and dynamic domain/history blocks; those remain unknown rather than being priced as empty.

#### KV Cache effect

Each actor retains its own canonical Session across residency changes. Replacing the prior current view changes that request prefix; accepted tool/message history remains append-only. A newcomer does not inherit another actor's Session or old channel history.



## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- Governance supports the installed unanimity and absolute-threshold rules. Closure neither enacts a norm nor proves that a Session consumed an announcement; arbitrary voting-policy scripts are unsupported.
- A stale plan after identity preparation retains its unresolved maintenance obligation; the trusted product owner must resolve or cancel it before ordinary scheduling resumes.
- The product scheduling owner provides explicit authenticated start/pause and recovery over the retained creation. The driver itself does not discover or open runs, publish pages, or own browser/SDK controls.

Paused external reconciliation also selects an unresolved original representative reservation saved before maintenance begins. It starts that reservation’s frozen maintenance request, prepares its exact canonical identity and commits the original accepted Persona without inference. Committed or abandoned reservations without unfinished maintenance are not reopened.

`ExternalMediatedWorldExecution` applies only the original accepted adjudication. Fact, report, failure and delayed outcomes use the Native mutation owner; finite representative preparation uses the same canonical reservation, maintenance and topology owner as ordinary external actors. The fixed director sends the safe result through the original requester control Channel, and an actually ready representative receives the requester’s separate first contact. Paused reconciliation completes only retained preparation responsibility and preserves explicit pause intent. Execution and cold reconciliation perform no new adjudication inference or outside-world IO.

Scenario-directed environment requests require an original finite calendar and an explicitly opted-in domain. Each actual occurrence records an eligible director wake or an exhausted independent `maxRequests` budget. Native derives the trigger, stable business key, scope and target from that source; copied IDs, role text and arbitrary wakes cannot confer authority. Accepted cases retain the actual Director Decision/binding/epoch and trigger with `notificationOperationId: null`. Their request, plan and result observations consume separate causal Native contexts through the same Agent tools and shared fair queue. They create no self-directed Channel/Envelope. Ordinary requests retain their real private notification and consumed recipient proof. Rejected or cancelled scene cases remain charged; one occurrence admits one business identity. Delayed environment follow-ups consume their original recorded leaf and result wake. Native stream 102 rejects earlier formats; compiler 9 records the independent scene budgets and declared installed capabilities.

Only a typed Native revision collision becomes a bounded coordination retry while running or a paused stop after recorded pause. Original tickets, wakes and accepted effects remain retained; unrelated IO/context failures still propagate.

The director context includes only validated, unrevoked planner outputs addressed to that director and the current request. Each exact output remains a required context block with its generation and Session source. The director explicitly chooses `generationJobId`; helpers do not submit mutations. Ready Native plans execute in acceptance-sequence order, skipping currently blocked plans while retaining their original responsibilities.

The registered `organization/rename-object/1` execution provider uses Native-only local eligibility for plain display objects. Unknown or overlapping reads and unsupported providers use the existing bounded canonical maintenance collector. Cancellation retains unfinished maintenance, and reconciliation reuses the original plan/result; it never upgrades the operation to arbitrary profile editing.
