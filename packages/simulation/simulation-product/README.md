## Registration and authorization

Callers must obtain each call through the principal lease; an opaque product ID grants no authority.

Late arrivals reject; `stopPeer` persists permanent failure and blocks further time progress in that generation. `syncNativeClock` retains the authenticated mailbox cut in the exact local Native run; Native retains the exact mailbox `sourceSequence` and SHA-256 of its complete original `LogArchive` prefix, then enforces the exclusive limit and permanent stops in drive, decision-wake collection and scheduler replay. A synced frontier of zero freezes even time-zero consumption; frontier five permits time four, and processing time five requires a later frontier. A frontier of `maxTime + 1` permits the final Native tick without changing Native’s own time bounds. Initial Native binding requires a paused run. Object migration, distributed resource settlement and consistent multi-partition checkpoints require their own owners and proofs. The [effect and mailbox decision](../../../.agents/notes/implemented/architecture/2026-10-20-world-effect-sandbox-mailbox.md) records the scope.

## Token recovery

World control reports the original `tokenRecovery` quote (reservation, actual usage, interval and required additional interval tokens) and `requestBudget` refusal measurements. A paused `runs-manage` request may carry that exact quote. The owner requires settled work and complete reported usage, atomically acknowledges the original overrun and adds only the quoted interval deficit, and keeps scheduling paused. A separate explicit start remains necessary and may still refuse other exhausted budgets. Read-only inspection and cold observations never restore ownership or invoke a model.

### Federated resource ownership and checkpoints

The opt-in `federation-ownership` service owns a separate federated credit object: one home, one monotonic generation and a bounded canonical quantity. Deployment supplies one coordinator principal, explicit partition principals, a private journal and finite entry/byte/page allowances. All spending passes through this coordinator; old generations and unreachable authority fail closed. `reserve` freezes the whole resource for both partitions, `confirm` records destination acceptance, and coordinator-only `settle` transfers home and increments generation. Unknown outcomes retain reservation without timeout release. Native resource balances are not mirrored automatically.

`captureFederationCheckpoint` acquires actual mailbox queues and quiescent Team connection collections in sorted run order, then the resource coordinator. It exports full Native/Team/Channel/Session/attachment archives, original mailbox journals and unresolved resource responsibility under aggregate bounds. `verifyFederationCheckpoint` checks versions, hashes, complete partitions and message/receipt references; isolated imports replay original owner parsers. `federationCheckpointStreams` selects original registry/mailbox bytes, while each partition's `world` contains its D1 archive. See [ownership and consistent-cut rationale](../../../.agents/notes/implemented/architecture/2026-10-20-world-federation-ownership.md). Coordinator replication, arbitrary Native resource transactions and production rollout remain unsupported.

Federation checkpoint validation ties every retained Native time fence to the matching mailbox owner, run, generation and `sourceSequence`. Its SHA-256 covers the exact original log prefix, while clock/stop facts come from the same pure reducer as the mailbox owner. A correct package hash cannot justify a forged Native limit or omitted stop. The mailbox may contain later records beyond a valid Native reference; missing or mismatched historical prefixes, including unclosed provenance dependencies, reject.
