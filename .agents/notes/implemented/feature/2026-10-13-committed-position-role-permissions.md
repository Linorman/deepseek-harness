# Agent Note: Authority follows committed position assignments

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-10-13-committed-position-role-permissions.zh.md)

## Problem

A declared position permission must follow the actual person holding that position. Preparing a new identity does not establish employment, and a confirmed private Channel alone does not establish a World requester index. A hired actor needs both the original committed role authority and canonical communication route to submit autonomous requests safely.

## Decision

Native installs position relations and operation grants from the original World declaration after a completed generated-hire, existing-hire or transfer business fold establishes its actual assignment and result. The shared pure installer validates the original accepted intent/plan, owned committed reservation or mutation, person binding, assignment and exact declared operation mapping. No model supplies a role, actor, relation or permission. Transfer keeps the deterministic `world-role-authority:<intent>:<index>` keys; hiring uses the same ownership rule without inventing personnel fences or generations.

Generated and existing hires index their exact already-confirmed canonical private director Channel in that same business append. Existing consistent indexes remain unchanged; foreign or ambiguous sources reject. Preparation grants no authority, and no second Channel or replacement identity is allocated.

Exact business retries return their original result and do not duplicate grants, pay again or reopen ended relations. Departure continues to close original derived role authority through the personnel owner. The shared code performs no IO and preserves the independent fee reservation/settlement logic. Native journal format 91 rejects earlier formats; the portable v1 compiler retains version 9 and its current shape.

## Alternatives considered

**Install authority during preparation.** An unresolved or cancelled identity could then act as an employee before its assignment and financial commit exist. The committed business record is the earliest valid authorization point.

**Let the model choose permissions or create another director Channel.** Names and model output do not prove authority or canonical sender ownership. The original position declaration and confirmed topology provide those proofs.

**Keep separate per-owner grant loops.** Hiring and transfer could drift in mapping and retry behavior. One pure installer validates the actual owned source while each business owner retains its commit and cleanup responsibility.

## Consequences

New employees can submit only operations declared for their actual position, through their original private Channel. A changed or foreign source blocks the complete append rather than exposing partial authority. Role relations remain historical after withdrawal; no automatic authority or identity substitution repairs malformed records.

Owning JSON and SQLite tests exercise both actual hiring owners: preparation exposes no derived grant, commit creates the original mapping, a real World request retains the canonical private post, simulated expiry refuses a new request, and exact commit retry preserves history. Foreign role relations and replacement result identities reject. The focused six-file regression has 103 passing tests. Whole-world actor behavior and cold fault recovery remain separate evidence; this change does not claim new charged-hire SIGKILL coverage.
